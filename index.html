Approach

A considered introduction, not a scattered application.

Every lender has its own appetite, sectors it favours and amounts it prefers. Our role is to understand your business first, then introduce you only to those best placed to help.

i.
Within one working day

Conversation

An adviser calls to understand the business, the requirement and the timing. No documents are needed at this point.

ii.
Following the call

Assessment

We identify which lenders on the panel are best suited to the business, its sector and the funding it needs.

iii.
When you're ready to proceed

Introduction

We introduce you to those lenders with the context already set out, so each conversation starts from an informed position.

On timing: unsecured loans can be offered within 24 hours of a complete application. Secured, asset and invoice finance usually take one to six weeks, as assets and ledgers are valued first.

A business's own bank is one lender among more than two hundred. It is rarely the one best placed for every requirement.

Lenders differ widely in what they will fund, how they structure it and how quickly they move. An introduction made with that in mind saves time on both sides and tends to lead to better terms than an application made in the dark.

Questions

Before we speak.

Does an enquiry affect our credit profile?

No. Enquiring with Vale Advisory places no search on the business's credit file or on yours personally. A lender will only carry out its own checks later, once you have decided to proceed and given your permission.

Is Vale Advisory a lender?

No. Vale Advisory is an introducer. We identify suitable lenders from our panel and introduce you to them. Each lender assesses the application against its own criteria and makes its own decision. We do not lend, make credit decisions or guarantee approval.

We already have facilities in place. Is that a problem?

Not at all. Most of the businesses we speak with already have an overdraft, a loan or some hire purchase. Part of the conversation is whether new funding should sit alongside it, or whether refinancing would leave the business better placed.

The requirement is some months away. Is it too early?

It is usually the right time. Secured, asset and invoice finance take several weeks to arrange because assets and ledgers are valued first. Beginning early means terms are considered rather than rushed.

Who sees the information we share?

An adviser reviews your enquiry and calls you within one working day. Nothing is passed to a lender until you have spoken and agreed. Thereafter, your details are shared only with the lenders we introduce you to and the service providers needed to process the application.
